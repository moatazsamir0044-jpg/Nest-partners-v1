## Phase 0 — Three Decisions Before Day 1

One management meeting unlocks the entire launch. Nothing else is blocked on leadership.

| # | Decision | Options / recommendation | Blocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Commission amounts (EGP)** for Sahel bookings and furnishing deals | Set flat EGP values per confirmed conversion; sanity-check against current paid-media cost per booking so referral CPB is clearly cheaper | All launch materials, orientation content, announcement copy |
| 2 | **CX inclusion path** | Option A (timestamp: form submission must predate any CRM record) recommended; Option B (manual novelty audit) or Phase-1 exclusion acceptable | Eligibility comms, form logic, validation SOP |
| 3 | **Launch date (Day 1) + programme name** | Pick Day 1 so Day 7 lands early in a week; confirm final name (working name: Nest Ambassadors; alternatives: Flock'd, Side Nest, Nest & Earn) | Everything downstream; branding on all assets |

> **Recommendation to accelerate:** Bring proposed commission values and Option A to the decision meeting as defaults so the meeting approves rather than debates. Treat V1 amounts as testable — they can be tuned at the Week-4 gate.

## Build Specifications (Day 3–4 Checklist)

### Referral Submission Form — required fields
- Ambassador full name and department (dropdown).
- Lead full name, phone number, and (optional) email.
- Product track: Sahel Reservation / Furnishing Package (single choice).
- How the Ambassador knows the lead (short text — supports dispute resolution and CX Option A/B).
- Auto-captured submission timestamp (the attribution source of truth — do not allow manual entry).

### Tracking Sheet — locked column structure
- **Auto from form:** timestamp, ambassador, department, lead name, lead phone, track.
- **Added by commercial ops:** validation status (new / duplicate / invalid), validation date, assigned to (Sales/CX), conversion status, conversion date, booking/deal value, commission due, commission paid, notes/dispute flag.
- One owner in commercial ops edits statuses; everyone else is view-only. Reconcile weekly before the WhatsApp leaderboard update.

### Referral codes & toolkit
- **Codes:** one per employee, format `NEST-[initials]-01`; issued with the toolkit and quoted by customers at booking.
- **Toolkit** (distributed Day 5): personal referral code, one-page product fact sheet (Sahel + Furnishing), WhatsApp message templates, referral form link.

## Weeks 1–4 — Operate, Track, Decide

| Window | Focus | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| **Weeks 1–2** — Pulse check | Track submissions, validation rate, first conversions; monitor Ambassador engagement; feature the first submission ("The first referral just came in!" — with consent); "Did you know?" content series | Weekly WhatsApp leaderboard update + weekly ops reconciliation |
| **Weeks 3–4** — Impact assessment | Evaluate confirmed bookings, furnishing leads in pipeline, and referral cost per acquisition vs. paid channels; mid-programme shout-outs; preview Gold & Silver rewards; countdown posts | Weekly update + assessment prep for the gate |
| **End of Week 4** — Decision gate | Scale or kill: if conversions justify cost, extend to the full Sahel season and expand the furnishing incentive — and begin scoping V2. If not, sunset with documented learnings | One leadership review meeting |

### Gate criteria (bring these numbers to the Week-4 meeting)
- **Activation:** > 30% of eligible employees submitted at least one referral in the first 2 weeks.
- **Quality:** validation rate high enough that ops load per good lead is sustainable; dispute rate near zero.
- **Economics:** referral cost per booking meaningfully below paid-media cost per booking; furnishing leads accepted into active Sales pipeline.
- **Momentum:** repeat referral rate and time-to-first-conversion trending positively.

## Capture Now for V2 (So the Vision Learns From V1)

These cost nothing extra during V1 but make the partner-platform business case write itself:

- Log ops time spent per referral on validation and follow-up — this is the automation ROI figure for the platform.
- Record every dispute and its resolution — this becomes the partner rulebook.
- Tag every conversion with its channel path (code quoted, form-first, etc.) — this defines attribution requirements.
- Note which employees behave like natural "partners" (volume + quality) — likely first recruits or internal champions for V2's trusted external circle.
- Keep the final commission values and their observed effect on motivation — the starting point for external commission design.
