# Nest Ambassadors — Department Task Ownership

**BirdNest · Internal · Confidential**
*Execution assignments per department — bring to the Phase-0 meeting · July 2026*

Every task below has one owning department, one named deliverable, and a day on the 7-day clock. "Done when" is the acceptance test — a task is not complete until its deliverable exists. Assign one named person per department in the meeting and write their name in the Owner column.

## Cross-Department Dependencies — the critical path

1. **L1–L4 block everything.** No commissions, no CX decision, no date → no materials, no SOP, no launch.
2. **O3 (codes) needs HR's staff list** → feeds M3 (toolkit) → feeds Day-5 distribution.
3. **S3 (fact sheet) feeds M3 (toolkit)** — Sales must deliver it by end of Day 4.
4. **O5 (validation SOP) needs L2 (CX decision)** — form logic and eligibility comms both wait on it.
5. **M5 (launch) needs L6 (leadership message)** — schedule the recording before Day 6, not on it.

**Meeting output required:** one name in every Owner column, the three Phase-0 decisions, and a confirmed Day 1.
