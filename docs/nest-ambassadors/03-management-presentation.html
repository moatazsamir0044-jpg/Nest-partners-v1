Strategic Context

The vision: a BirdNest Partners ecosystem

The CCO's direction: build a partner-driven referral engine — brokers, freelancers, property owners, eventually companies — that refers customers to BirdNest and earns commission automatically, the way Nawy Partners did for real estate and Carofi Partners did for auto finance in Egypt.

First break the vision down to a goal we can hit this week — prove the model works with the people we already trust, before we build anything.
Core Reasoning

Why we don't build the platform first

Economics

Unknown unit economics

We don't yet know conversion rate, cost per booking vs. paid media, or the commission that actually motivates referrers.

Operations

Unproven ops muscle

Validation, disputes, payout timing, and Sales handoff have never been run. V1 builds these manually first.

Timing

Season pressure

Sahel's peak window is ~90 days. A platform build misses it entirely; a form-and-sheet programme captures it.

Risk

Trust & control

Employees are a known, coachable referrer base — fraud and brand risk are far easier to manage while rules are stress-tested.

Programme Overview

Nest Ambassadors — how it works

Track A

Book a Nest — Sahel Reservations

New customer only, never booked with BirdNest. Commission on checkout confirmed + full payment + no cancellation. Paid post-season (Sept 2026).

Track B

Furnish a Home — Furnishing Packages

New client only, not already in Sales pipeline or CRM. Commission on deal closed + full payment received. Paid on confirmation.

Both tracks: share a unique referral code or link → earn cash commission on confirmed conversions → top performers unlock bonus incentives. Employee discounts do not apply to referred deals.

Special Consideration

The CX question — solved, not avoided

CX handles all inbound leads. Without a mechanism, agents could claim commission on any lead they touch — making the programme economically unviable. Two options:

Option A — Recommended

Pre-submission before contact

Form timestamp must predate any CRM record of the contact. If the lead appears in CRM first, the claim is invalid. Clean, enforceable, zero tech.

Option B — Alternative

Dedicated CX track

Only contacts with zero prior BirdNest touchpoints; agent submits a personal note; Sales manager verifies novelty in CRM. Clear audit trail, more manual effort.

Benchmark Analysis

What Nawy and Carofi teach us

Nawy Partners 6,500+ partner companies

Free onboarding, dashboard beyond commission, express-payout speed as retention lever, contests & recognition — and an internal "ambassador" mode, mirroring what BirdNest is building as V1.

Carofi Partners auto finance, Egypt

Proves the partner model generalises beyond real estate — but productised the partner experience only after manual commission mechanics existed.

  • Both companies layered a platform on top of proven manual economics — BirdNest's V1-first approach copies the sequence, not just the end state.
  • Explicit attribution and payout rules are non-negotiable in both — our V1 rulebook is the seed of that.
  • Payout speed and public recognition are cheap, proven engagement levers we're already building in.
Risk Management

Risks — named and mitigated upfront

Attribution disputes

Mitigation: tracked-before-contact rule, timestamp as source of truth, Commercial Director as final arbiter.

Post-season payout fatigue

Mitigation: weekly "commission locked" confirmations, visible leaderboard, faster First Blood/streak payouts as morale fuel.

Low activation

Mitigation: leadership launch message, 45-min orientation, ready-made toolkit, First Blood urgency.

Lead quality dilution

Mitigation: new-customer-only validation, lead-quality criteria in training, validation rate tracked weekly.

CX resentment

Mitigation: transparent framing of the attribution problem + Option A as the fairness bridge.

Sheet chaos at volume

Mitigation: locked columns, single ops owner, weekly reconciliation — a good problem that triggers V2 tooling.
